Pathlight

A personal safety app designed to help users feel more aware, connected, and confident wherever they go.

```

Pathlight provides quick check-ins, nearby community safety updates, easy reporting tools, and a simple safety dashboard so users can stay informed and share their status with trusted contacts.

Features

  • Quick Check-In: Let trusted contacts know you are safe with one tap.
  • Safety Map: View nearby safety updates and reports.
  • Community: Stay connected with friends and trusted contacts.
  • Easy Reporting: Report concerns or incidents quickly.
  • Safety Dashboard: Access key safety tools from one simple home screen.

Privacy

Pathlight is designed with user safety and privacy in mind. The app only uses information needed to provide safety features such as check-ins, location-based updates, and reporting.
```
